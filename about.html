About Us

Crafted for beautiful living.

OAK & ASH makes furniture that feels timeless, comfortable, and built to last. Each piece is designed with care and made by hand in small batches.

View collection Our story
Scroll

We build furniture with intent.

From the first line drawing to the final finish, our process combines thoughtful design with skilled craftsmanship.

We source premium oak and ash, shape each component by hand, and finish every surface so it feels warm and natural in the home.

Our story

Since our founding, we have grown from a small workshop into a brand known for thoughtful furniture and warm, approachable design.

2018

Founded

Our first studio opened with a focus on solid joinery and handmade oak furniture.

2019

First Collection

We launched our debut collection of tables, seating, and casegoods for modern interiors.

2021

Expanded Nationwide

Our studio grew and we began shipping to customers across the country.

2024

International Delivery

We introduced international shipping and began partnering with boutiques abroad.

Mission

To create furniture that balances quiet luxury with everyday ease, using materials chosen for beauty and durability.

Vision

To be recognized for modern craftsmanship, thoughtful materials, and pieces that feel both meaningful and effortless.

Craftsmanship at every step

We combine traditional woodworking techniques with modern finishes. Every board is selected for stability, every joint is checked for strength, and every surface is sanded by hand.

Design with purpose

Each piece is shaped to feel balanced and livable. We start with proportion and refine details so furniture works beautifully in the room.

Material-driven style

Natural grain, warm textures, and simple silhouettes create a calm foundation that complements both modern and traditional interiors.
